Project Nimbus — Internal Notes

Project Nimbus is an internal tool for tracking weather-sensitive deliveries.
It was started in March 2024 by the logistics platform team and is written in
Python with a PostgreSQL backend.

Architecture
The service has three parts. A collector polls the national weather feed every
fifteen minutes and writes observations to the "observations" table. A scoring
worker joins those observations against scheduled deliveries and assigns each
delivery a risk score from 0 to 100. A small web dashboard shows dispatchers
the highest risk deliveries for the next 24 hours.

Risk scoring
A delivery scores above 70 when wind speed exceeds 45 km/h, when freezing rain
is forecast within two hours of the delivery window, or when the destination
depot has reported a closure. Scores are recomputed every time new observations
arrive, so a score is never older than fifteen minutes.

Operations
Nimbus runs on three application servers behind a load balancer. The on-call
rotation is weekly and handover happens Monday at 10:00. The most common
incident is the weather feed timing out, which the collector retries three
times with exponential backoff before raising an alert.

Limitations
Nimbus does not cover international shipments, and it has no mobile app. The
team plans to add depot-level historical accuracy reporting in the next
quarter.
